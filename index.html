🎧 Voice Reels Review

You'll see a transcript and hear an audio clip. Judge whether the audio matches the transcript:

👉Swipe right — ✅ Correct (audio matches the text)
👈Swipe left — ❌ Very bad (doesn't match / garbled)
👆Swipe up — ⏭️ Skip
✏️Tap the transcript to correct it — type in English letters and your keyboard converts to Telugu (Gboard). Telugu script only.
▶️Tap Play (or press Space) — audio loops so you can correct while listening

⚠️ Please review honestly and carefully — false or random answers will be excluded. Reviewers with accurate reviews earn a bounty.
